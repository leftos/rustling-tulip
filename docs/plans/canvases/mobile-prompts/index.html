Session prompts: how a pending prompt shows in the chat, and what it leaves once answered

Settled already (RT-143): chat above the status line, the live terminal from it down (so Claude's own dialog is always drawn there too), and mobile-session lane C, where the dock between the terminal and the composer shows a pending prompt's options as big buttons that type the option's number. Open here: what the chat shows for the prompt the hooks report (pending_input: a permission's tool and summary, a question's options, a plan's text), and what stays after the answer. Each lane shows a permission, an AskUserQuestion with three options and Other, a plan approval, and the chat after all three were answered. Frames start below the status bar. Content is a placeholder.

A. Card in the chat, buttons in the dock

The chat ends in a card that says what is asked in full (the whole command, the question's option notes, the plan as Markdown) and carries no buttons; the dock below the terminal holds the answers, exactly as mobile-session lane C drew it. Answered, the card becomes a one-line receipt a tap reopens.

Worst case: the prompt shows three times (card, terminal dialog, dock buttons); a four-option question's dock takes about 250px and leaves the card under 190px, so a plan card is clamped to "Show whole plan".

B. Buttons on the card, keys-only dock

The pending tool call's own card opens into the prompt: what is asked, then its options as buttons that type the option's number. The dock keeps only Esc, ↑, ↓ and Keys. Answered, the card shrinks back to its tool row with a note of the answer. This revises mobile-session lane C, whose dock held the buttons.

Worst case: the buttons scroll with the chat, so a scroll back to reread takes them off screen; a card left in history must refuse a late tap once its prompt is gone.

C. A bottom sheet over the chat

The prompt rises as a sheet over the chat, the terminal and the composer: what is asked, the answers as buttons that type the option's number, Esc ↑ ↓ under them, and a Terminal toggle that shows Claude's dialog as drawn. A swipe down puts it away behind a "Needs you" bar. Answered, a quiet line stays in the chat.

Worst case: while open it hides the live terminal and most of the chat you may need to decide; a second prompt arriving swaps the sheet's content under your thumb.
