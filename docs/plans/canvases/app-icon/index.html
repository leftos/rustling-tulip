App icon concepts

Five tulip concepts in the client's own palette, on a dark rounded plate. Each lane shows the concept at 160 px, then at 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px on a dark and a light taskbar (the sizes the .ico carries). The last lane is the current placeholder.

A · Bloom

A single red tulip on a green stem. The plainest mark; holds its shape at 16 px.

B · Prompt

The bloom with a terminal prompt cut into it. The prompt drops out below 32 px and leaves A.

C · Bouquet

Three tulips from one stem: many sessions from one app, in the blue, red and amber the status colours use. Turns into a colour cluster at 16 px.

D · Petals

One tulip whose petals are separate colours, each one a session. Brighter and more logo-like than A.

E · Rustle

The bloom leaning in a breeze, with wind lines: the name's "rustling". Off-centre, so it reads smaller at 16 px.

Round 2 · refining B

Chosen: B1, with the 16 px glyph for the smallest size (RT-410). Round 1 chose B. These lanes vary its framing. The 16 px slot in each light row shows a glyph drawn for 16 px (bloom wider, prompt on whole pixels) rather than the large art shrunk; the dark row keeps the shrunk art for comparison.

B1 · As drawn

Round 1's B: bloom, leaf and stem on the plate.

B2 · Large bloom

No stem; the bloom fills the plate, so the prompt holds down to 24 px.

B3 · No plate

The tulip alone on a transparent ground, as most Windows app icons are; larger at every size, but it depends on the taskbar behind it.
