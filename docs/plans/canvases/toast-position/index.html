Toast position

Toasts are the app's own windows, placed on the monitor where the app's window is, inside its work area (clear of the taskbar or Dock). Today they always sit at the bottom right. The newest is nearest the corner and older ones stack away from it. This mock shows a setting that picks one of eight anchors around the screen's edge, numbered clockwise from top left, and how the stack grows from each. The owner chose lane A's picker, eight spots with no centre, and lane C's stacking.

APicker: a monitor with nine spots

Settings › Notifications. Click a spot on a small monitor drawing; the caption names it. "Show a test toast" shows a sample toast there and stays on screen until it closes. Like every other setting, the choice waits for OK or Apply.

CHow three toasts stack at each spot

The blue-edged toast is the newest. Top-row spots stack downward and bottom-row spots stack upward, each newest nearest its edge. Middle-row spots (middle left, middle right) keep the newest on the middle line and grow downward. If a stack doesn't fit, the oldest Finished toast drops first, as today.
