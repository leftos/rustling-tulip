Phone home: what the app shows when no session is open

The screen the PWA opens to, and the one a tap on its Home Screen icon lands on. Same moment in every lane: nine sessions on LEFTOS-PC across two repos (rustling-tulip, yaat) and one workspace (harness = claude-config + gate). Two need you (a permission prompt and an AskUserQuestion), one finished while you were away, three working, three idle. The Home Screen badge counts Needs you only, so it reads 2 in every lane. A tap on any session opens the session screen already settled (the dock follows the prompt). Statuses and words are the desktop's: Working, Needs you, Finished, Idle. Content is a placeholder.

A. Collie's list: grouped by repo, rows never move

One group per repo or workspace, in the sidebar's order, each row in its fixed place. Urgency is a mark, not a position: an amber wash on the row, a dot and count on its heading, and one summary line on top that counts what needs you and jumps to the first. Every answer happens in the session screen.

Worst case: with twenty sessions the second Needs you row sits three groups down, off screen; answering anything is always two taps and a screen change (open the row, press in the dock).

B. Attention feed: answer from the card

Sorted by what needs you: Needs you cards on top, each with the prompt's summary (the file and diff line, or the question) and its options as buttons; a tap types the option's number, as the session dock does. Then Finished with Claude's last line, then Working, then Idle folded.

Worst case: you answer from a summary without the terminal in sight, and a status change reorders the feed, so a card can slide under your thumb as you reach for it (needs a freeze while touching); two prompts fill the first screen.

C. Hybrid: a Needs you tray over collie's list

A's grouped list, rows fixed in place, with a tray on top while anything needs you: one compact prompt card at a time, swiped sideways through the rest, with the options as a row of short buttons. A question whose options do not fit in one row shows Open instead. The tray goes away when nothing needs you.

Worst case: the tray spends about 170px whenever something waits; option labels are cut to a word or two, and an AskUserQuestion with long options always costs the trip into the session.
