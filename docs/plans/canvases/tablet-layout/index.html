Tablet and landscape layout: options

Sizes are in points at half scale: iPad 11" (1180×820) and iPhone 17 Pro (874×402). Each layout follows the window's width, not the device model.

Question 1: iPad layout

A · Two-pane (phone parts side by side)

The phone's Home list stays docked on the left, and the selected session's screen (chat, with the Terminal toggle in its header) fills the right. Same views and rules as the phone, laid out side by side. One session at a time; no tabs.
